🛡️ FinCrew: Cashflow Quest

Game-a-thon 2026 · Track 3 FinTech · PS 3.1 · Team: Ram, Sumit, Masoom · SDG 8 · 9 · 16

1. The Idea (one paragraph)

FinCrew is a co-op strategy game where you are the Captain of a small shop, and your AI finance crew defends it for 90 simulated days. Four crew members (agents) watch invoices, payroll, taxes and bank activity. Surprises hit as waves: a client pays late, a big expense lands, a fraud attempt arrives, a growth opportunity appears. Crew members propose moves as Action Cards. You approve, reject or let them auto-play under a limit you set with the Trust Dial. Every move prints a Why-Receipt into a tamper-evident Black Box log.

Hook: a Ghost Twin runs the same 90 days with no crew, side by side. The gap between your business and the ghost is the proof the solution works, shown live on one screen.

GamifiedExplainableOwner-in-controlMeasurable

The crew (the 4 agents = 4 playable "classes")

📬 Collector

Watches invoices. Predicts which client will pay late (from past delay pattern), drafts reminders, offers early-pay discount. late payments

🧮 Treasurer

Forecasts cash runway 30/60/90 days. Schedules payroll, tax and bill deadlines, warns before a gap, suggests which bill to delay. deadlinescash flow

🕵️ Sentinel

Flags suspicious transactions (duplicate invoice, changed bank account, odd amount, odd hour). Triggers the Fraud Boss minigame. fraud

🚀 Scout

Spots growth chances (bulk order, supplier discount, hire). Runs "can we afford it?" simulation before recommending. growth

Owner's key question answered: "What do I need to know, and when?" → one daily Morning Briefing (max 3 items, ranked by ₹ impact and deadline) plus red alerts only when something is urgent.

3. Why it's novel & unique

👻 Ghost Twin (counterfactual)

Most fintech demos show a dashboard. We show what would have happened without us, live. It's the proof judges want, and it's cheap: run the same sim twice with a "crew off" flag.

🎚️ Trust Dial

Per-agent autonomy: Suggest only → Ask first → Auto under ₹X. The owner earns trust gradually, like leveling up. Answers "keep the owner in control".

📦 Black Box + Replay

Hash-chained log (each entry hashes the previous). Scrub back in time and replay any decision with its Why-Receipt. Answers SDG 16 (accountable institutions).

🔮 Risk Radar (look-ahead)

Shows a "danger fog" over next 14 days on the timeline: red days where cash dips below payroll. You react before the wave, not after.

🕹️ Fraud Boss minigame

20-second spot-the-fake: two near-identical invoices, find the red flags with Sentinel hints. Makes fraud awareness fun and teachable.

🌱 Real-world mode

Same engine accepts a CSV of real invoices/expenses. The game is a front end for an actually usable product (stretch goal, shown as 1 slide).

8. Collision-proof rules

  • Folder ownership: you only edit your folders. Need a change elsewhere? Message the owner, don't edit.
  • Git: branches ram/*, sumit/*, masoom/*. Merge to main only at Gates (H2, H6, H11, H18) and by Ram. Pull before you start each block.
  • Contracts frozen at H2. Add fields only (never rename/remove) and announce in chat.
  • Mocks: /content/mocks.json has sample State and Cards so UI and ledger work without the engine.
  • Shared files (index.html, main.js) owned by Sumit; others add one import line via message.
  • Check-in every 90 min: 5 min standup: done / next / blocked.
  • Single source of truth: only engine mutates state; UI and ledger just listen on the bus.

10. Judge Q&A counters

Is it just a game, not a real solution? The engine is a real cashflow/obligation model; the game is the interface. It takes a CSV of invoices and expenses (stretch slide) and outputs the same cards.
Where's the AI? Agents use rules + simple prediction (late-pay risk from client history, anomaly checks). Claude only rewrites explanations; if offline, templates run. So it never breaks and decisions stay explainable, not black-box.
How does the owner stay in control? Trust Dial per agent, approval cards, spending cap, every auto action reversible within the day, full log.
How is the record trustworthy? Each log entry hashes the previous one (SHA-256). Editing any past entry breaks the chain and the UI shows "tampered".
How do you prove it works? Ghost Twin: same seed, same surprises, crew off. Health and cash gap is the measurement. Reaction speed is tracked in days.
What catches risks like late payments / missed deadlines / suspicious activity? Collector (late), Treasurer + Risk Radar (deadlines), Sentinel (fraud). Each maps to a wave type.
What's novel? Ghost Twin counterfactual + Trust Dial progression + hash-chained Why-Receipts, packaged as a game.
Why would a small business use it? Zero setup, runs in browser, free, no bank login, plain language, 3-item daily briefing.
Privacy/security? All data stays in browser. No backend. LLM call (optional) gets only anonymised amounts and labels.
Business model / scale? Free tier for MSMEs, paid bank/accounting integrations (Tally, UPI, GST APIs), bank partnerships for fraud signals.
Limitations? Simulated data, rule-based predictions. Next: real integrations and ML on user history.

Tech choices

Vanilla JS ES modules, HTML5 Canvas, CSS. No framework, no bundler, no backend (open index.html with a tiny static server). Emoji/SVG art keeps asset time near zero. Seeded RNG (mulberry32) makes demo deterministic. Hosting: Netlify drag-and-drop.
